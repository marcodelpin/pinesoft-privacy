Privacy Policy

App: GiroGruppi
Developer: PineSoft
Last updated: September 29, 2026

GiroGruppi builds balanced groups from a list of people you enter and rotates them over sessions. It works entirely on your device.

Summary

GiroGruppi does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. PineSoft receives nothing from your use of the app.

Offline, No Account

The app works fully offline. It has no sign-up or login, and it does not request any permission, including internet access.

Data Stored on Your Device

The names, tags, groups and settings you enter are saved in a local file inside the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Sharing and Backup

GiroGruppi shares data only when you ask for it, through your system's share sheet:

  • Backup: a JSON file with all your data (girogruppi-backup.json).
  • Export: a CSV file or plain text with the groups you built.

You choose the destination app. That app, not PineSoft, receives the data and handles it under its own privacy policy. A backup is restored by pasting its text into the app.

No Ads, No Analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Children

GiroGruppi does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your Control

Because your data stays on your device, you control it completely: edit or delete entries in the app, export a backup, or delete everything by clearing the app data or uninstalling.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
