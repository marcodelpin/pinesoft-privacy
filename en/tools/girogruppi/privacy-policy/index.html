Privacy Policy

App: GiroGruppi
Developer: PineSoft
Last updated: October 1, 2026

GiroGruppi builds balanced groups from a list of people you enter and rotates them over sessions. The people, groups and history you enter stay on your device.

Summary

The people, groups and history you enter stay on your device. There is no account and no PineSoft server, and PineSoft receives none of that data. The app shows one banner ad from Google AdMob, and Google's advertising SDK collects some device data, as described in the Advertising section below.

No Account, No PineSoft Server

The app has no sign-up or login and does not send your data to any PineSoft server. Your lists and groups work without a connection. The app uses internet access only to load the ad and, if you buy it, to process the Remove ads purchase.

Data Stored on Your Device

The names, tags, groups and settings you enter are saved in a local file inside the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Sharing and Backup

GiroGruppi shares data only when you ask for it, through your system's share sheet:

  • Backup: a JSON file with all your data (girogruppi-backup.json).
  • Export: a CSV file or plain text with the groups you built.

You choose the destination app. That app, not PineSoft, receives the data and handles it under its own privacy policy. A backup is restored by pasting its text into the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

GiroGruppi by PineSoft shows one banner ad provided by Google AdMob. Only non-personalized ads are shown.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, consent is collected through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can review or change your choice at any time from the "Privacy options" entry in the app menu.

To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following data from your device and shares it with Google:

  • advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address, from which approximate location may be derived;
  • app interactions, such as ad impressions and taps, and diagnostics.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit. PineSoft does not receive it.

How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps

Remove Ads Purchase

The optional Remove ads purchase is processed by Google Play. PineSoft receives no payment data.

Children

GiroGruppi is not directed at children under 13, and PineSoft does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your Control

Your lists and groups stay on your device, so you control them: edit or delete entries, export a backup, or delete everything by clearing the app data or uninstalling. You can change your ad consent from the "Privacy options" menu entry and reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
